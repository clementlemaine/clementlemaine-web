Privacy policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

In short: this site asks you for no data. It has no forms, no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no trackers.

Data controller

Clément Lemaine, Madrid (Spain). Contact: cm.lemaine (at) vatel.com.es

What the site does not do

  • No forms and no account creation.
  • No audience measurement (no Google Analytics or similar), no ads, no social media pixels.
  • No cookies (see the cookie policy).
  • Nothing loaded from other sites: the fonts are hosted on the site itself.

Hosting: GitHub's logs

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub receives your device's IP address and technical information (browser, page requested) in order to send you the pages. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. This processing is GitHub's, under its privacy statement, and may involve a transfer to the United States. I have no access to these logs and do not use them.

If you email me

If you send me an email, I receive your address, your name if it appears, your message and any attachments.

  • Why: only to reply to you, for example about an internship or a job.
  • Legal basis: my legitimate interest in replying to messages and, when you contact me about an opportunity, steps taken before a possible contract (Art. 6(1)(f) and 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Retention: for the time of our exchange, then at most 3 years after our last contact. You can ask me to delete it sooner.
  • Recipients: nobody but me. The mailbox is provided and hosted by my school (Vatel).

Light or dark theme

If you switch themes, your choice is saved in your browser and never sent anywhere. See the cookie policy.

Links to other sites

Links to LinkedIn or other sites open services with their own privacy rules. Nothing is loaded from those sites unless you click.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or erase your data, restrict or object to its processing, or receive it in a portable format, by writing to cm.lemaine (at) vatel.com.es. I reply within one month.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority: the AEPD in Spain, where I live, or the authority in your own country.

Changes

If the way the site handles data changes, this page and its date will be updated before the change.
