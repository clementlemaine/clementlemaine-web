Cookie policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

This site sets no cookies, neither its own nor any other site's. That is why there is no consent banner: it would have nothing to ask you.

ItemDetails
Namecl-theme (browser local storage, not a cookie)
PurposeRemembers the light or dark theme you chose

DurationUntil you clear this site's data in your browser

Why no consent is requested

The rules on trackers (Article 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive, Article 82 of the French Data Protection Act, Article 22.2 of the Spanish LSSI) exempt what is strictly necessary for a service you explicitly request. The French (CNIL) and Spanish (AEPD) authorities list remembering a display choice made by the user among those cases.

Content from other sites

The site loads nothing from other sites: no Google Fonts, no embedded videos, no share buttons. The mobile preview shows this same site. Links to LinkedIn or other sites only open those services if you click, and their own cookie rules then apply.

Deleting your theme choice

Clear this site's data in your browser settings. The site will go back to the default light theme.

If this changes

If I ever add a tool that needs it (analytics, embedded video…), your consent will be asked before anything is stored, and this page will be updated.
