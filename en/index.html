AI tool · DRM Fishing Store, Madrid

From invoice to product page

  1. The problemThe owner created every product page by hand on his online shop: title, description, photo, category, stock. Hours lost with every delivery.
  2. The solutionAn app on his phone. He takes a picture of the invoice, a barcode or the packaging: the AI identifies the product, writes the page, cuts out the photo and prepares the draft in PrestaShop.
  3. The safeguardNothing goes live without his approval, and a product already in the shop only gets its stock updated.
